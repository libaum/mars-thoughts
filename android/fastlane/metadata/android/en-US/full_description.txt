Mars Thoughts is a place for thoughts to land before they disappear.

It opens straight into a blank editor with the cursor blinking. You type, you leave, it's saved — no titles, no folders, no save button. Not notes, not a wiki, not a second brain. Just somewhere to catch a fleeting thought with as little friction as possible.

Open. Type. Close.

Features:
• Opens directly to writing — the cursor is already waiting
• Auto-save: leave the editor and it's kept; empty drafts are discarded
• Three panels: swipe between Pinned, Write, and All thoughts
• Long-press to pin the thoughts you keep coming back to
• Swipe to delete, search to find anything
• Light and dark mode — double-tap anywhere to switch
• Pure black and white UI — calm and clutter-free

Offline and private. Your thoughts never leave the device. No ads, no tracking, no account needed.
